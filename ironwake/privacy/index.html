Privacy

Applies to iOS 1.0.0 (34). Updated 17 November 2026.

Progress files

Export and import are optional and local. Imported totals keep the higher values. Imports retain original data in local backups until app data is removed. Exported files are not encrypted; keep them somewhere safe. No files are uploaded by the game.

About this policy

Ironwake is an offline tank defense game. This notice describes the bundled iOS and Android game. The public website, app stores and operating-system services may have their own privacy notices.

What stays on your device

The game stores your current expedition, best score, completed-run totals and settings in local WebView storage. Settings include language, sound, motion, step controls and menu text size. There is no player account or developer-operated cloud save.

Network and third-party software

Gameplay, artwork and synthesized sound are bundled with the app. The game code has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, purchases or account SDKs and does not send your saved progress to the developer. Capacitor provides the native WebView wrapper. Android declares Internet permission for that wrapper; permission alone does not mean that game data is uploaded.

System backups and diagnostics

Device backup, migration, restore and operating-system or store diagnostics may be handled by Apple or Google according to your device settings and their policies. Android app backup is allowed in the current configuration. These services are separate from developer-operated collection; this notice does not promise that system services never process data.

Retention and control

Local progress remains until replaced, removed by the operating system, or cleared when app data is removed. Starting a new expedition replaces the expedition checkpoint. An unreadable checkpoint is kept until you deliberately replace it. Removing app data can permanently erase progress; there is no developer recovery server. System backups may retain copies under your system settings.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notification access. It contains cartoon tank combat. Refer to the App Store listing for the age rating; this is not a Kids-category release.
