Contact

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, selected language and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Progress files

Open Camp and Progress files to export or preview an import. Replacement requires confirmation. Raw recovery exports preserve original data but cannot be imported as playable progress. Seed challenges use separate temporary sessions and do not replace the expedition or its records.

Saved progress

Pause and choose Save & camp before leaving a battle. Playing progress is saved about every five seconds and on pause or backgrounding when storage is available. A warning means progress may not be saved. Keep the app installed if you need to preserve progress.

If a checkpoint cannot be read

The original checkpoint is kept. Avoid starting a new expedition if you want it investigated, because that replaces the checkpoint. Do not clear app data as a troubleshooting step when you need your progress.

Controls and readable menus

In Camp options you can enable Step movement buttons, select Reduced motion or Use system, and choose 100%, 150% or 200% menu text. Menus also allow pinch zoom. The arena keeps its direct touch controls and full-screen camera.

Reporting a problem

Record the app version, phone model, OS version, weapon and wave, and what happened before the problem. Screenshots are useful; avoid including personal information. No report is sent automatically.
