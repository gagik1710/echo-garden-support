Privacy

Applies to iOS 1.10.0 (26). Updated 17 November 2026.

Your game, on your device

No account or internet connection is needed to play. Scores, discovered questions, active games, team or player names, settings and turn deadlines are saved on your device to resume games and display results. You can use nicknames. The game does not send these records to us.

Advertising and tracking

This version has no advertising, paid purchases, accounts or integrated analytics service. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Links and sharing

Read more opens an external website only when you choose it. That site may receive network information such as your IP address and follows its own privacy rules. Share result passes the result and included names to the app or recipient you select. Review the text before sending it.

Storage and backups

Game data stays on the device until replaced or removed. The operating system may include it in backups or transfer it to a new device according to your settings. Apple and Google backup and diagnostics services follow their own policies. The game has no separate cloud synchronization service.

Managing your data

Reset in Progress clears solo progress, but does not clear team games. Clearing all app storage on Android, or deleting the app on iOS, removes local game data. Offloading an iOS app keeps its data. Manage backups and previously shared results separately in the relevant service. Your language choice is saved separately from game progress.

When you contact support

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed through your email provider and Gmail, the support mailbox provider, under Google’s privacy policy to answer your request and troubleshoot the problem. Do not send sensitive information. You may ask for deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the same address. We keep correspondence only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.

This website and platform services

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting under its privacy statement. These pages contain no added analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or forms. Following an external link is subject to that website’s policy. Apple and your operating system separately provide backup, diagnostics and distribution services under their settings and policies.
