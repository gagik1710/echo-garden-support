The clock keeps running

Leaving the app does not pause an active timed turn. The clock has not started on a handoff screen. The Daily Five changes at midnight in Armenia (Asia/Yerevan), with a separate deck and record for each language.

If you need help

Return Home and choose Continue to resume a saved game. If a question or answer seems wrong, note its topic, answer and clue. When reporting a problem, include the app version below and your device model. Do not send personal information or real team names. Publisher: Gagik Khalatyan. For support or privacy questions, email gagikkhalatyan25@gmail.com.

Protect your progress

Do not reset progress or delete the app as the first troubleshooting step. Reset in Progress clears solo progress, not team games. Deleting the iOS app removes local data; offloading keeps it. System backups are managed separately, and recovery is not guaranteed.

Offline play and languages

No account is needed. Questions are bundled. Choose Armenian, Russian or English at first launch, or change the app language from Home settings. Read more opens a source in your browser. Sharing results is optional: check the included team or player names before sending.

Purchases and restoration

History, Movies and Music are free. Other topics use one optional permanent purchase, not a subscription. The App Store shows the price for your country or region before payment. Open a locked topic or Home → Settings (gear) → All topics. Restore Purchases is on the unlock screen; use the same Apple Account that bought it and connect to the internet. Purchases belong to the Apple platform and are not transferred to Google Play. Cancelling or pending approval does not unlock paid topics. A verified purchase is required; if the store is unavailable, try again later. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot a purchase or send us your Apple password.
