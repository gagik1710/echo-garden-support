How to play

Each direction moves the seed now. Water repeats the previous direction; a blocked move still counts. The flower blooms when the seed reaches its flowerbed and water reaches the marked pool at the same time. Use the guide and path descriptions to understand switches and bridges.

Progress and stars

Complete gardens in order to unlock the next. Improve your best move count to earn more stars. Undo returns the previous move. Restart resets the current attempt, not your saved best.

Your saved garden

The app stores best move counts, extra-chapter results, language, motion and seasonal preferences, music choice, flower placements, paths, decorations and encountered visitors on this device. Unfinished attempts are not restored after the app process closes. There is no app-operated account or cross-device sync. System backups may restore earlier data.

Saving problems

If a save warning appears, keep the app installed, check available device storage and try again. Reinstalling or clearing storage can remove progress; these are not repair steps.

Settings and sound

Choose English, Russian or Armenian in Settings. Gentler motion is available; system Reduce Motion takes priority. Musical echo is optional and plays while the game is open.

GIF postcards

After completing a garden, use the postcard action to create an animated GIF. On iOS, choose a destination in the system share sheet. The game writes a temporary file to its cache; the destination you choose handles the shared file under its own terms.

Report a problem

Contact gagikkhalatyan25@gmail.com. Include app version/build, device and iOS version, garden number and reproduction steps. Screenshots are optional. Do not send passwords, private files or unrelated personal information. No report is uploaded automatically.
