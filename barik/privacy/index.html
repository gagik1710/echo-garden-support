Barik Word Game

Privacy policy

Information used by the game

Barik stores your chosen language, progress, paused rounds, stars, daily results, discovered and favourite words, character outfits and sound settings locally on your device. No game account is required. The game has no advertising, in-app purchases or app-operated cloud-sync service. Words, illustrations and synthetic pronunciations are bundled for offline play.

The game does not send your gameplay progress to the developer and has no integrated advertising or user-analytics service. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access for gameplay.

Progress copies and external links

Settings can prepare a text copy of your progress. You choose where to keep or share it. The game does not automatically send the copy to the developer. Importing a compatible copy requires reviewing its summary and confirming an additive merge. Language and sound preferences are not included in the text copy.

Cultural-source links open other websites in your browser. Your game progress is not appended to those links. The operating system may separately include app data in backup or device-transfer features, depending on your settings.

Managing local data

The confirmed reset in the game clears active progress across language collections, but does not erase every preference or retained legacy migration copy. Removing the app and its data through system controls removes its local data; system backups may retain or restore an older copy. Keep a progress copy before removal if you wish to preserve it. There is no game account to delete.

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed through your email provider and Gmail, the support mailbox provider, under Google’s privacy policy to answer your request and troubleshoot the problem. Do not send sensitive information. You may ask for deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the same address. We keep correspondence only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.
