Protect your progress

If progress fails to load, try Retry first. Do not delete the game or reset progress as the first troubleshooting step. Removing the app can remove local progress; recovery from a system backup is not guaranteed.

Settings can prepare a text copy of progress. Keep it somewhere you control. To import, paste a compatible copy, review the summary and explicitly confirm the merge. Language and sound settings are not included. Do not post a progress copy publicly.

Full Barik on iOS

iOS version 1.21.0 introduces optional Full Barik access. The first world in each language, tutorial, daily puzzle and review of discovered words are free. One non-consumable purchase unlocks the other worlds in that version for all three languages. Normal level progression still applies. There is no subscription or advertising. The purchase screen shows the App Store price for your storefront and currency.

Open Settings > Full Barik to buy or restore. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and connect to the internet. Unlocked content can be played offline. If the product or price cannot load, retry later; do not delete the game or reset your progress. A progress text copy is not proof of purchase.

Existing installations with eligible pre-purchase local saves can retain full access on that installation. This local grant is separate from an App Store purchase and is not guaranteed to survive deleting local data. Do not buy again if the game already shows full access. For purchase problems, send the app version and error message to support; never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Sound and offline play

Words, pictures and synthetic pronunciations are included in the app. Check the game’s sound controls and device volume if audio is missing. When reporting pronunciation issues, include the language and word.
