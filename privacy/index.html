Echo Garden: Bloom — Privacy policy

Русский

Publisher and scope

Gagik Khalatyan provides Echo Garden: Bloom for iOS. For privacy questions, contact the public support mailbox listed on the support page. Last updated: 7 November 2026. This policy covers the installed game. Support website and email processing are described separately below.

Local game data

The game saves best move counts, chapter scores, language, reduced-motion preference, season, music preference, flower placements, path/decorative choices and encountered visitors on your device. Stars and unlocked gardens are derived from progress. An unfinished attempt remains only while the app process is running.

Playing offline

The puzzles, artwork, sound and help are bundled with the game. There is no app-operated sign-in, advertising, in-app purchase or cloud-sync service. The game application code does not upload scores, garden settings or accessibility preferences to a publisher server.

Analytics and tracking

The publisher has not configured an advertising, tracking or gameplay analytics service in the game. The app does not have a publisher-operated server for collecting game progress or preferences. Sharing services, email support, website hosting and Apple system services are described below and operate under their own practices.

Postcards you choose to share

If you create a GIF postcard, the app generates it locally and writes a file to its cache. The iOS share sheet lets you choose whether and where to share it. A chosen recipient or service receives the file under its own practices. Cached files can remain until the system clears the cache or app data is deleted.

Device access

Gameplay does not request camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location permissions. System accessibility settings are read to respect reduced motion and screen-reader use. The game does not request advertising tracking permission.

Backups and diagnostics

iOS and Apple services may process device backups, transfers or diagnostics under system settings and Apple policies. The publisher does not operate the device backup service. A restore can bring back earlier local game data.

Retention and removal

Local scores and preferences remain until app data is removed. Deleting the app removes local game data; offloading an iOS app may retain it. Manage system or cloud backups separately. There is no Echo Garden account to delete.

Support messages and website

If you email the public support mailbox, Gmail receives the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Google processes Gmail communications under its own privacy policy. Gagik Khalatyan has access to support messages and uses them to respond to your request. We plan to delete support messages and attachments from the support mailbox 90 days after resolving your request. This does not set Google’s own retention periods. The support website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when they are not signed in. These static pages contain no publisher-added analytics, advertising, forms or third-party embeds.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when the app or its handling of information changes. For questions or requests about your support messages, contact the public support mailbox on the support page.
