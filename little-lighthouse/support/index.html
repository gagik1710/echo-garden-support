Saving and updates

Update the existing app rather than uninstalling it. Progress is local, with a recovery backup of a previous valid save. There is no online account or automatic cross-device sync. Recovery after deletion is not guaranteed.

If progress appears missing, stop and contact support before reinstalling, clearing data or repeatedly starting a new game. Describe the last version used and what happened before the issue.

Sound and controls

Check game audio settings and device volume. For a control or layout issue, describe your device orientation and the screen where it occurs. If the game closes unexpectedly, include what you were doing immediately before it closed.
