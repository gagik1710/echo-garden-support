Little Lighthouse: Keeper

Privacy policy

Local game information

The game stores language, sound and reduced-motion settings, tutorial progress, best score, shells, rescued ships, decorations and equipment, lighthouse style, collections, daily-task dates, achievements and the last result locally on your device. Internal run identifiers prevent rewards from being counted twice. The device’s local date supports daily tasks and rewards.

No game account is required. The game has no app-operated server, integrated advertising or user-analytics service, real-money purchases or in-game cloud-sync service. Shells are local game currency earned through play, not a payment account. Gameplay does not require camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Saving and removing data

The game maintains a local save and a backup of a valid previous save. A legacy local save may be read during an update. These files are not an online account and do not automatically synchronise between devices.

Local progress remains while app data is available. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove progress, and the game has no account-based restore. A local recovery file is not the same as a system or cloud backup. System backup and device transfer depend on the platform and its settings; the game does not guarantee recovery. Do not uninstall as the first troubleshooting step if preserving progress matters.
